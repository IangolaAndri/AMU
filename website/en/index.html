Malagasy Association of UdeM

AMU is a community founded in August 2024, bringing together students from all academic levels, as well as Malagasy researchers and professors at the Université de Montréal.

Our History

Founded in August 2024, AMU was created with the goal of uniting students and members of the Malagasy community at the Université de Montréal. What began with a small, motivated group hoping to build a space for connection, support, and cultural exchange has since grown into a dynamic association. AMU continues to evolve thanks to the dedication of its members, while staying true to its mission: building a strong, inclusive community proud of its roots.

Our Mission & Values

Our mission is to create an environment where every member feels welcomed and supported. We value solidarity, respect, and openness, and we aim to promote personal and academic growth within our community. AMU is a place for mutual support, cultural exchange, and collective empowerment — open to students and non-students alike.

Our Objectives

We aim to facilitate integration into academic and social life, organize enriching cultural and academic activities, share helpful resources, and promote Malagasy culture in Montréal. Every initiative is designed to strengthen community belonging and build a supportive, friendly network.
